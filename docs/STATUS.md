## Findings from building the extractor (2026-09-18)

1. **li1 `x/16` shapes are markers, not pins.** In these GDS files they are 0.17 um squares
   at the labels. A pin is the cell-internal conductor under its label.
2. **Pins span several islands.** sky130 pins can be a met1 strap over li1 islands
   (`xor2_2` B, `dfrtp_2` RESET_B) or two li1 islands on one gate poly (`a31oi_2` A1, whose
   LEF port lists both rectangles). Routers land on any island. In the puzzle, the router
   routed *through* an `a31oi_2` A1 gate: `and2_2` X and `nor3_2` C on one island,
   `a311o_2` A1 and the cell's own A1 on the other. Following metal only, **both
   extractors** reported an undriven net there. The V4 diff (KLayout showed an unnamed pin)
   exposed it; following poly through licon fixed it in both, and V2 now checks every pin
   against its LEF rectangles.
3. **Tie cells reach the supplies through poly resistors.** Following poly, `conb_1` HI
   merged into VPWR and LO into VGND. Poly is now cut at the resistor marker `66/15` (only
   `conb_1` uses it), in both extractors.
4. **15 unloaded `clkbuf_4`** sit on clock nets (5-6 flop CLK pins each) with nothing on
   their output: OpenROAD CTS dummy loads. Benign; the test pins the count at 15.
5. **Pixel art on met2.** 1366 0.3 um squares (in the warm-up too) extract as 3 floating
   met2 islands that touch no signal (easter egg, stretch goal S2).
6. **The sample trace prints "TRY AGAIN".** After `enable` falls, `O` emits 0x54 0x52 0x59
   0x20 0x41 0x47 0x41 0x49 0x4E, one byte per cycle, twice (two runs of 121 enabled cycles
   each). **Coverage caveat:** `success` never rises in the trace, so V6 does not exercise
   the success path. V4 (two extractors) is the main evidence for that logic.

## What the chip is (sprint 3, `docs/INTENT.md`)

An 11 x 11 **Star Battle** ("Two Not Touch") checker. `enable` runs a base-11 x base-11
counter over 121 cells (one bit of `I` per cell, row-major); the design then decides
once, on the 121st enabled cycle, and prints a message on `O`.

| Block | Flops | Function |
|---|---|---|
| counter | 9 | column digit `lo` and row digit `hi` (0-10 each), plus sticky `cnt_done` |
| array | 44 | 22 two-bit saturating bins: 11 irregular region masks (group A), 11 column decodes on `lo` (group B) |
| left_top | 16 | 12-cell shift register of `I` (the current neighbourhood) and two sticky error flags: `hist_hit` (neighbouring stars) and `row_count_err` (set at a row's last cell unless the row holds exactly 2 stars) |
| left_bottom | 8 | count of `I = 1` cells (8 bits, frozen after `cnt_done`) |
| check | 3 | `armed`, and the two outcome latches `success` and `alt` |
| outgen | 12 | message ROMs and a scrambler (LFSR over `I`); prints 9 bytes after the decision |

**`success` rises iff, at the decision cycle:** exactly 22 stars were entered
(`left_bottom` = 22), every region bin and every column bin holds exactly 2,
`row_count_err` = 0, and `hist_hit` = 0. With stars touching but everything else right, the
`alt` latch is set instead and `O` prints "TWO NOT TOUCH". Other messages: "EMPTY SKY"
(0 stars), "BIG BANG" (all 121 cells), otherwise "TRY AGAIN" (the sample trace). On
success, `O` prints a table XORed with the scrambler, so the readable text depends on the
bits entered.

**Lead-review corrections to the agents' drafts:** the required count is 22, not 11
(check.v compares the raw flop-id pattern `0000_1011`; with the counter's real bit
weights that is 22, confirmed by simulating the proven counter), and "BIG BANG"'s raw
`0xAE` is the count 121. The V7 harness also had a bug that left the gold `O` bus
undriven (fixed; the outgen reviewer found it). See INTENT.md §3 and §6.1.
