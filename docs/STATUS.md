## Finding (resolved): the puzzle was built with open_pdks `8afc8346`

`tools/retrace/cellcheck.py` compares every `sky130_fd_sc_hd` master in a design GDS
with a PDK's cell GDS, layer by layer (polygons normalised on a 1 nm grid,
independent of order and start vertex).

Against the first pinned version (`0fe599b2`, the OpenLane 2 default), 66 of 69 masters
matched. `o211a_2` (poly, licon), `and4b_2` (poly) and `conb_1` (95/20) differed.
Scanning 24 of the 116 released sky130 versions (standard-cell library only, fetched with
`ciel fetch -l sky130_fd_sc_hd` on the mini) found two families:

| Versions (list position, newest = 1) | Result |
|---|---|
| 1, 13, 25, 37, 49, 50, 51, 52 | `and4b_2` and `o211a_2` match; `conb_1` differs on 66/15 |
| **53: `8afc8346a57fe1ab7934ba5a6056ea8b43078e71`** | **all 69 masters identical** |
| 54-116 (sampled: 54-60, 61, 62 = `0fe599b2`, 68 = `bdc9412b`, 70, 73, 85, 97, 109, 116) | `and4b_2` and `o211a_2` differ; `conb_1` on 95/20 or matching |

**No tampering.** The differences were PDK revisions. The puzzle and the warm-up
(18/18 masters) both match `8afc8346` exactly, so it is now the pinned PDK: enabled on
the mini (`~/pdk-sky130`) and copied to local `pdk/`. Against `0fe599b2`, its
`sky130_fd_sc_hd` LEF, tt Liberty and `primitives.v` are byte-identical, and
`sky130_fd_sc_hd.v` only *adds* `sky130_ef_sc_hd__decap_*`/`fill_2` modules, so the
logic cell models are unchanged.

## Findings from building the extractor (2026-09-18)

1. **li1 `x/16` shapes are markers, not pins.** In these GDS files they are 0.17 um squares
   at the labels. A pin is the cell-internal conductor under its label.
2. **Pins span several islands.** sky130 pins can be a met1 strap over li1 islands
   (`xor2_2` B, `dfrtp_2` RESET_B) or two li1 islands on one gate poly (`a31oi_2` A1, whose
   LEF port lists both rectangles). Routers land on any island. In the puzzle, the router
   routed *through* an `a31oi_2` A1 gate: `and2_2` X and `nor3_2` C on one island,
   `a311o_2` A1 and the cell's own A1 on the other. Following metal only, **both
   extractors** reported an undriven net there. The V4 diff (KLayout showed an unnamed pin)
   exposed it; following poly through licon fixed it in both, and V2 now checks every pin
   against its LEF rectangles.
3. **Tie cells reach the supplies through poly resistors.** Following poly, `conb_1` HI
   merged into VPWR and LO into VGND. Poly is now cut at the resistor marker `66/15` (only
   `conb_1` uses it), in both extractors.
4. **15 unloaded `clkbuf_4`** sit on clock nets (5-6 flop CLK pins each) with nothing on
   their output: OpenROAD CTS dummy loads. Benign; the test pins the count at 15.
5. **Pixel art on met2.** 1366 0.3 um squares (in the warm-up too) extract as 3 floating
   met2 islands that touch no signal (easter egg, stretch goal S2).
6. **The sample trace prints "TRY AGAIN".** After `enable` falls, `O` emits 0x54 0x52 0x59
   0x20 0x41 0x47 0x41 0x49 0x4E, one byte per cycle, twice (two runs of 121 enabled cycles
   each). **Coverage caveat:** `success` never rises in the trace, so V6 does not exercise
   the success path. V4 (two extractors) is the main evidence for that logic.
