# S3 — structure recognition in anonymous gate-level netlists, and its blind evaluation (PRD S3)

**What this is.** RETRACE's S3 slice finds counters, shift registers, LFSR/CRCs and synchronizers — and a word
grouping — in a netlist stripped of every name, and a harness then tries to *prove* each answer on its own copy
of the netlist. `docs/S3_DESIGN.md` is the design and the specification; this is the report its §4.4 asks for.
The code, the truth files it can see, the evaluation protocol and a 128-bit draw seed were frozen in commit
`232cfe6` (2026-09-23 06:35:58 UTC). Ten third-party Tiny Tapeout designs were then drawn with that seed,
labelled by 06:50:45Z, and run between 06:53:52Z and 07:29:11Z the same morning; the puzzle was run separately,
07:30:45Z to 07:30:56Z. Nothing in `tools/s3/` changed at any point:
`.venv/bin/python -m tools.s3.freeze check` prints `freeze holds`.

**The answer in one paragraph.** On the blind set the recognizer found **59 of 94** structure-kind registers
(0.628), and the harness certified the matching structure for **41 of the 94** (0.436) — that is 41 of the 59 it
found. It returned 409 structures and claimed 215 of them "proven"; the harness **adjudicated only the 78 of a
scored kind** — proving 40 and refusing 38 — and never checked the other 331, on which 137 of the 215 claims
sit (§7). Only `counter`
carries enough support to be called a rate: 59 registers — coincidentally the same 59 as the pooled found count
above, and a different set — over all 10 designs, found 41 (0.695), harness-verified 23 (0.390).
That verified figure is **below** the
pre-published out-of-sample estimate of 0.636 (Fisher's exact two-sided p = 0.017), and verified *exact* recall
(15/59 = 0.254 against 0.636) is below it by a margin nothing marginal explains (p = 0.00013); the
all-structures found rate is not distinguishable from its reference at this support (p = 0.060). **Every one of
those verdicts compares two different item sets** — `score.py`'s register denominators on the blind side,
registers *plus* declared units on the holdout side — and `out/s3/honesty/holdout_rates.py` says of its own two
aggregations that they are "NOT comparable"; no **BELOW** or **INDISTINGUISHABLE** verdict below may be read

further three came back **not separable** rather than matched — the all-structures counter rate (p = 0.060),
`synchronizer` in every one of §6's three tables (p = 1.000, 1.000 and 0.143, against a reference of six items)
and certified parameter accuracy (p = 0.26, §12.1) — and every remaining comparison in §6 is BELOW.

But the match is for `counter` only: the

while the blind set's one shift-register structure

## 1. The problem, the recognizer, and what "verified" means

The recognizer (`recognize(nl, params) -> result`) sees only a `Netlist`: library masters, pin names, Liberty
functions, integer nets, ports in a random order, constants. It never sees RTL, names, placement or the truth.
It must return structures — disjoint flop sets with a kind, a bit order, parameters and a statement of the
control conditions under which each holds — plus a partition of every flop into words. `tools/s3/run.py` pickles
the netlist, runs the recognizer as a subprocess under an in-process guard and an OS sandbox, and asserts that
no truth name appears in what it was given.

**The point of the slice is the next step, and it must be read before any number below.** A structure's own
`proof.status` is the recognizer's *claim* and counts for nothing. For each of the four scored kinds
`tools/s3/verify.py` (contract v2.1) **builds the defining template itself** from the structure's kind, bit
order and parameters, on the harness's own graph of the netlist; the recognizer supplies only the control
conditions, and for `lfsr_crc` one XOR expression per flop. A restatement of a flop's own D therefore never
verifies anything. Four obligations bound what a verified structure can be:

| Obligation | What it refuses |
|---|---|
| **Extent** | a counter of width < 2; a modulus or saturation limit that leaves a claimed bit dead by range; a shift register of depth < 3 or of unequal lanes; a synchronizer of < 2 stages |
| **Liveness** | a counter bit that cannot move — `D ∧ (template bit xor its q)` must be satisfiable for *every* claimed bit, so a structure cannot be padded with passengers |
| **Coverage / priority** | each named reset case is checked on its own priority cube, so a case a higher-priority one shadows is vacuous and refuses the structure |
| **Hold** | `control.hold` is *required* for a counter and a shift register, and its region is decided by SAT, not by syntax. An empty hold region is accepted only when the cubes the harness actually **checked** cover it (`HOLD_EMPTY_NEEDS_VERIFIED_COVER`); an opaque `load` case may never be the thing that empties it, because a load case costs no evidence |

So *verified* means the harness proved this kind's template on this netlist. It does **not** mean the truth
agrees: verification is a statement about the netlist, not about anyone's register list. §7 shows 13 verified
blind structures that earned no credit at all, two of them LFSRs proved over registers the frozen labeller calls
shift registers — the authors named both `lfsr` (§10, D1). **Found and verified are reported apart, per kind, everywhere in this document**, and the
harness outcome that explains each found-but-unverified register is given in §9.2.

**One trap in the comparison, stated once.** The corpus holdout's `found` column is **already a harness-verified
rate** — its own aggregation string reads "found = matched a harness-verified structure of an accepted kind at
IoU > 0.5; exact = equal flop sets and verified". It therefore pairs with the blind set's **verified** column,
not with its all-structures column. Where an all-structures comparator is wanted, this report **derives** it as
`(found + found_unverified) / items` from the document's own columns and labels it derived;
`holdout_rates.json` publishes no such rate. The two item sets are also not the same object. The holdout's own
aggregation string, its item clause in full: "one item per structure-kind truth register, **except a
synchronizer register of a declared chain unit, which is counted through its unit**, plus one item per declared
structure-kind unit" (the found/exact half of the same string is quoted five lines above). The
blind figures are `score.py`'s register denominators. That exception is not decoration: it is why the holdout's
synchronizer row is 6 items (§6.1) while `honesty_table.json` counts 10 synchronizer registers over the same
40-design split. §1 and §6 use the short form of the clause; this is the clause.
`out/s3/honesty/holdout_rates.py` says of its own two aggregations that they
are "NOT comparable", and this cross-set comparison inherits that caution. **That caution is not a footnote: it
applies to every BELOW and INDISTINGUISHABLE verdict in §6.**

A second frozen artifact narrows the gap and is quoted beside the derived comparator in §6.2.
`out/s3/honesty/honesty_table.json`, force-added in the same commit, publishes corpus-holdout rates on
`score.py`'s **own register denominators** — counter 36 found and 26 verified-found of 40 registers,
`shift_register` 16 and 16 of 26, `lfsr_crc` 14 and 14 of 14, `synchronizer` 8 and 8 of 10 — which are the
denominators the blind columns use, and whose `found` column is the all-structures rate the derived comparator
has to stand in for. It is a *sibling* evaluation, not the same run as `holdout_rates.json`
(`honesty_table.json`: seed 20260923, 80 evaluations; `holdout_rates.json`: seed 20260922, whose holdout arm is
also 80 runs — the 336 is its `source.runs`, the whole corpus evaluation the holdout was cut from — over the
same 40-design holdout split), so this
report keeps `holdout_rates.json` as the pre-published reference of record and prints the `honesty_table.json`
figures alongside rather than in place of it.

## 5. Blind results: registers found and verified

**Strict and lenient are numerically identical on all 10 blind designs**, in every count checked — registers,
structures, found, exact (lenient differs only by carrying an `excused_registers: []` key that strict omits). On
the puzzle they agree on every found and exact figure and differ only in the total match count — 33 strict
against 34 lenient, because lenient also scores the declared `fsm_state` unit "decision control (armed +
success_latch + alt_latch)" as an item and a `data_register` structure matched it. **On TEMPO they do not
agree:** `truth_tempo.ALT_KINDS` excuses 3 registers in lenient mode (2 `counter`, 1 `shift_register`), so the
excused counts and the excused-adjusted recalls printed beside the recalls differ — verified `counter`
`recall_without_excused` 0.4091 strict against 0.4500 lenient, verified `shift_register` 0.5000 against 1.0000.
No headline recall, precision, F1, found or exact count moves on TEMPO either. That is exactly the labelling
asymmetry the rest of this paragraph describes, and on the blind set it is not a coincidence and it is not good
news. Denominators coincide by
construction (`Truth.denominators`: `alt_kinds` never shrink a denominator), and no blind register of a scored
kind carries `alt_kinds` at all, because `thirdparty.py` takes `alt_kinds` only from the design-agnostic
classification rules while `truth_tempo.ALT_KINDS` — the five hand-written entries recording TEMPO's own label
ambiguities — is keyed by TEMPO register-name regexes and can never fire on a third-party design. Only 4 blind
registers carry `alt_kinds` anywhere (all `flag` with a `counter` alternative from the 1-bit toggle rule), and
`excused` is 0 in all 40 design-kind cells. **The blind set is scored under a harsher labelling convention than
the development design**, and every table below is both modes at once rather than printing one column twice.

## 8. Every miss, with its cause

35 registers of a scored kind were missed. All 35 are accounted for by eight causes, and the per-kind totals
reconcile exactly with the frozen scorer's own blocks.

| Cause | n | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **M1** | 5 | partial extent: a genuine counting word, narrower than the truth register, IoU ≤ 0.5 |
| **M2a** | 2 | kind error, extent exact: the whole register is one structure of an unscored kind |
| **M2b** | 10 | kind error with a grouping split: the register is spread over several unscored structures |
| **M3** | 1 | merged with a neighbouring register; one-to-one matching gave the pair to the other one |
| **M4a** | 2 | affine-feedback handoff: `shift.py` routed the lane to `lfsr.py`, which proved an `lfsr_crc` |
| **M4b** | 5 | the same handoff, and `lfsr.py` admitted nothing |
| **M5** | 9 | truth kind `lfsr_crc` on a CPU register file; the recognizer holds at most 2 of 8 flops |
| **M6** | 1 | a synchronizer of one stage, refused by the frozen contract |

### 8.1 counter — 18 misses of 59

**Five are partial extent (M1)**, and three measurable mechanisms produce the narrow word. The first is the
enumeration frontier: `tools/s3/params.py EXHAUSTIVE_WIDTH = 12`, and every narrow Reloj sub-word is *exactly
12 flops*, as is the word over vzayakov's `VSCounter` (bits 8–19 of 20 — found at IoU 0.60, but not exact),
while carry-chain growth — which is what produces a wider
word — reached 26 flops on four other Reloj counters, all of which were found. The second is dead low bits under
a step of 2^k: `hourmod.hour` has truth step 10000 = 625 × 16, so its low 4 bits cannot move, and the recognizer
reports truth bits 4–15 with step 625 = 10000 / 2^4 — the arithmetic agrees exactly, and `verify.py` refuses a
counter with a dead bit, so those 4 flops could not have been in any verified word. The third is 32-bit RTL
`integer`s whose top bits never reach their toggle point; 8 of Reloj's 14 counters are 32 bits wide in the
truth. Two of the five fail **by equality** — `PWM_Generador.DIV_FREQ.counter` (3 of 6 flops) and `note_counter`
(4 of 8) sit at IoU exactly 0.50 and the matching rule is strictly greater.

**Twelve are a kind error (M2)** — for every one, the best structure of a scored kind over the register has IoU
0. (Not the same twelve as the cross-cutting twelve below; they overlap in 6 registers.) Nine of the twelve are
4 flops wide or narrower. Two recover the extent exactly and get only the kind wrong
(M2a): `td4`'s `cpu.pc` and basilisc's `cpu.pref.num_flushed`, both at IoU 1.00 as one `data_register`; both are
loadable words, which is what the counter recognizer's domain rules push out. The other ten are shattered into
1-bit `flag` structures and small `data_register`s. The recognizer's candidate-rejection histograms name the
dominant reason per design — basilisc: 447 candidates rejected as "every count step lands where the word holds
(a flag enables the count)"; pwm_top: 36 "other transitions in the domain" and 18 "a wrap that does not close a
count cycle" — but those are **design-level candidate counts, not per-register attributions**, and this report
does not claim otherwise.

**One is a merge (M3).** In `tt05/tt_um_toivoh_synth`, a harness-verified 21-flop counter holds all 16 flops of
`oct_counter` *and* all 5 of `state`. Matching is one to one: it was credited to `oct_counter` (IoU 0.76) and
`state` got nothing. In the netlist the truth's two registers are one counting word.

**A cross-cutting split worth reading beside the causes — and it is a different twelve.** Of the 35 blind
misses, **12 had their extent essentially recovered by a structure whose kind the truth refuses** (matched at
IoU > 0.5, credit refused only on the kind): **10 by a structure of an unscored kind** — `data_register` in
every one of the ten, which the harness therefore never checked — and **2 by a structure of a scored kind the
truth does not accept**, the two harness-verified `lfsr_crc` over truth `shift_register` registers of §10's D1,
both at IoU 1.00. **23 were matched by nothing at all** (best IoU ≤ 0.5). The twelve are a labelling failure
with the grouping right; the twenty-three are a grouping or extent failure as well. This twelve spreads over six
causes — 1 M1, 2 M2a, 4 M2b, 2 M4a, 2 M4b, 1 M6 — so it shares only **6 registers** with the M2 twelve above
(2 M2a + 4 M2b); the M2 twelve is 2 M2a + 10 M2b and is defined by the *absence* of any scored-kind structure
over the register, this one by the *presence* of an unaccepted-kind structure over it. The two counts coincide
at 12 by accident.

### 8.2 shift_register — 7 misses of 8, one mechanism

`tools/s3/shift.py` makes a feedback lane a shift lane **only when the head is not GF(2)-affine in the lane's
own bits**; every affine-feedback chain is handed to `tools/s3/lfsr.py`. All 7 misses took that exit. On
`ttsky26c/…_cordic`, `shift.py` recorded **3 distinct feedback heads of 19 stages each** — 6 relation records,
each head recorded twice — verdict `affine`, head + 19 = 20 flops, an
exact fit to the 3 truth registers of 20 flops — and `lfsr.py` then reported `lfsr_structures: 0` ("no strongly
connected affine mode could be modelled"). On basilisc, **2 distinct feedback heads of 7 stages** (head + 7 =
depth 8) — 4 of its 23 relation records; the other 18 distinct heads carry 3 stages — against the **4 lanes** of
the 2 missed truth registers, each 2 lanes × depth 8; `lfsr_blocks: 2`, `lfsr_structures: 0`. On the two designs whose
authors named a register `lfsr`, `lfsr.py` *did* prove one — as an `lfsr_crc`, which the truth calls a
`shift_register` (M4a; §10, disagreement D1).

**The control case is inside one of the netlists.** basilisc's `cpu.pref.sreg` has the identical shape to the
two missed registers there — 2 lanes × depth 8 — but its heads are input pins (`ui_in_reg[0..1]`) rather than a
feedback loop. It was found exactly and harness-verified; `cpu.pref.pc` and `cpu.pref.imm_reg` produced no
structure at all. Same design, same depth, same lane count; the head is the whole difference. These
identifications are **by shape**: the relation records carry head counts and stage depths, not flop names.

### 8.3 lfsr_crc — 9 misses of 9, one design and one labelling rule

All 9 are basilisc's CPU register file (`…general_registers.regs[0..7]` and `…sp_register.regs`, 8 flops each),
labelled by `truth_tempo.py`'s rule "own Q bits of other indices reach D through XOR (GF(2) feedback)", which
fires when ≥ max(2, n/4) bits have such feedback — an ALU that shifts and XORs its register file satisfies it on
every word. All of the truth's `PARAMS` for those 9 are null: no form, no polynomial, no `k_steps`, no bit
order. The recognizer split each 8-bit word into four 2-flop pieces, each piece straddling two words, so its
largest overlap with any of the 9 is **2 of 8 flops, IoU ≤ 0.25**. Relabelling them (§10, D2) would recover
none: the arithmetic settles that without a counterfactual run.

### 8.4 synchronizer — 1 miss of 18

basilisc's `ui_in_reg` is labelled a synchronizer with `stages: 1`. `verify.py SYNC_MIN_STAGES = 2` refuses that
by definition, so the freeze cannot report or verify it. The recognizer emitted a 2-flop `data_register` over
exactly those flops at IoU 1.00 (§10, D4).

## 9. Every false positive, and the found-but-unverified registers

### 9.1 33 false positives

| Cause | n | of which **harness-verified** | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F1** | 15 | **1** | a counter **returned** over a truth **accumulator** (matched, wrong kind) |
| **F2** | 10 | **7** | a counter returned over a fragment of a truth accumulator (matched nothing) |
| **F3** | 6 | **3** | a counter returned over a sub-word of a truth counter (matched nothing) |

**"Returned" is not "proved", and the verified column is why the distinction is in the table.** 13 of the 33
false positives are harness-verified — the same 13 of §7 bullet 3 — and the other 20 are structures the
recognizer returned and the harness **refused**. The split was taken from each record's own `verify.structures`
block (`verified`, `bucket`) over the ten blind records, not from the analysis artifact: `misses.json`'s cause
*names* say "proved" for all four causes, which is right only for F4 and understates the refusals on F1–F3.

31 of the 33 turn on counter extent or on constant-versus-variable step, and 25 lie on truth registers of kind
`accumulator` — the truth's rule for which is "D = Q + a multi-bit variable (the adder on own Q)", exactly the
distinction a counter template is meant to keep. **The harness did not draw it.** Of those 25 counter structures
over a truth accumulator (F1 15 + F2 10) it **verified 8** — 1 in F1 and 7 in F2 — and refused 17, 16 on `hold`
and 1 vacuous, against a base refusal rate of 38 of the 72 counter structures it saw. Nor is a refusal a
judgement about kind: both buckets are the counter hold obligation (§9.2), which never asks whether the step is
constant. So the 14-of-15 agreement on F1 is a coincidence of that obligation and not the harness ratifying the
truth's accumulator rule — 7 of the 10 F2 structures sit over the same truth kind and verified. The two F4 cases
are the verified LFSRs of §8.2.

Some events are charged twice by construction and this report does not net them out: each M1 partial-extent miss
also produces an F3 false positive (6 structures against 5 registers, because `hourmod.hour` contributes two),
and each of the two LFSR/shift disagreements is both a shift_register miss and an lfsr_crc false positive.
35 + 33 is not 68 independent errors.

**No miss or false positive anywhere is caused by a dropped, unmapped or shadow flop.** Only one design has
unmapped flops, all 7 fell in structures of unscored kinds, and no scored structure anywhere lost a flop (§4).

### 12.3 Order and bit level

**Order.** Every found counter of width ≥ 3 was ordered perfectly: 40 items, 2,797 ordered pairs, Kendall
score **1.000 at coverage 1.000** against a chance of 0.500, 40 of 40 "all correct". That is the blind figure
that meets its out-of-sample reference most cleanly, on 2,797 ordered pairs and with the two caveats below, and
it has a published out-of-sample
reference to meet: `out/s3/honesty/honesty_table.json` gives the corpus holdout's counter order as 38 ordered
items, 2,236 pairs, concordance **1.000** at coverage 1.000 against the same chance of 0.500, 38 of 38 all
correct. **That reference is pooled over both width bands, so the comparable blind figure is too.**
`honesty_table.py`'s `quality()` sums `score.order.summary[kind]` over every band — `width<=2` as well as
`width>=3` — across the arm's 80 evaluations, and its rows count matched truth **items** and not structures.
They are the **lenient** matches: `_core` feeds `_order` the lenient match list (`score.py:1658`, `:1659`),
while the `found` column `honesty_table` pools is the strict one (`_honesty`, `score.py:1569`). Lenient
`_items` (`score.py:766–778`) accepts a register on its kind *or* its `alt_kinds` and adds **every** declared
unit rather than only the chain units (`if lenient or u["chain"]`) — and on this arm it is the second difference
alone that opens the gap. Row by row over the arm's 80 evaluations, the whole excess of 2 is one design,
`cat_hilo_8x8_en_ar`, once per library: its truth declares a non-chain `counter` unit, "{hi, lo} as one counter",
over two 8-bit registers, so a 16-bit structure matches the unit while overlapping each register at an IoU of
exactly 0.50 — which the `> 0.5` rule refuses — and the row is ordered while no register is strictly found.
`alt_kinds` contributes nothing here, because `_order` files every row under the item's own truth kind
(`score.py:1161`), so the split's one `flag` register carrying a `counter` alternative lands in the `flag`
summary and never in the counter one. That is why that arm's 38 ordered items exceed its own 36 strict found
counter registers. Pooled the same way over the ten
blind records, `score.order.summary.counter` gives **41 items, 2,798 pairs, concordance 1.000 at coverage 1.000
against a chance of 0.500**, 41 of 41 all correct. The blind set matches the reference on either reading — the
width ≥ 3 band alone (40 items, 2,797 pairs) or both bands (41, 2,798) — and on the blind side the two readings
of "item" cannot come apart for `counter`: no blind register of a scored kind carries `alt_kinds` (§5) and all
ten declared units are synchronizer chain units (§11), so the blind 41 is strict-equivalent and equals the 41
found counter registers of §5.1, while the reference 38 is not strict-equivalent.

**Two caveats attach, the same ones §6 attaches to every other cross-set comparison.** The pairs are clustered,
not independent: the 41 items lie in 9 of the 10 designs (`ttsky25a/tt_um_td4` supplies none) and
`tt03p5/tt_um_Reloj_top` alone supplies **1,449 of the 2,798** (52%), with vgaclock 426 and cordic 292 against
6 from pwm_top and 4 from basilisc. And the figure is conditional on being found: it orders the 41 counter
registers the recognizer found of 59 (0.695), against a reference arm that found 36 of 40 (0.900), so the blind
order rate is measured over a more selected subset than the reference's.

**The match is for `counter` only.** On the same holdout `shift_register`
was ordered 20 of 20 at concordance 1.000 over 328 pairs against a chance of **0.6283** — not the counters'
0.500, because `shift_register` is one of `score.REVERSIBLE`; the blind set's one found `shift_register` asserted
47% of its pairs (score 0.733), below `ORDER_COVERAGE_MIN`, so it does not count as ordered at all. **That is an
untested case, not a refutation.** Being reversible, the kind is scored `max(C, D) + U/2`
because direction is a convention, and basilisc's `cpu.pref.sreg` row reads concordant **0**, discordant **56**
of 120 pairs: every pair the structure asserted agreed with the truth up to reversal. What fell short is

holdout's shift-register order result. The two found synchronizer **items** of width ≥ 3 — Reloj's

### 12.4 Permutation spread — and how many digits are meaningful

The protocol requires K ≥ 5 independent `os.urandom` permutations inside each attempt, because the recognizer is
**not proven** permutation invariant. Across all 11 blind/known records, 55 recognizer runs, **zero headline
metrics varied**: on the blind 10, of 972 metric slots 761 were defined in all five permutations and **0 varied**
(211 were undefined in all five, having neither support nor a prediction); on the puzzle, 92 defined, 0 varied.
No metric was defined in some permutations and not others. `spread.distinct_answers = 1` on every record, and
pairwise structure-set Jaccard is 1.000 min/mean/max on every design with 0 unstable members — **vacuously so
on `ttsky25a/tt_um_td4`**, whose scored-kind structure set is empty in all five permutations
(`spread.structures.per_evaluation = [0,0,0,0,0]`, `common = 0`, `union = 0`; `run.py`'s `_jaccard` returns 1.0
on an empty union), so nine blind designs and the puzzle are what carry that half of the claim. On those, the
five permutations agreed on every structure's kind, flop set, order, parameters, harness verdict class and
grouping. td4's own AMI of 1.0000 was identical across all five permutations, so the grouping half is not
vacuous there either.

That invariance is mechanistic rather than five lucky draws: the recognizer's canonical-order refinement left
`tied_cells = 0` and `tied_nets = 0` on all 11 designs, so no part of any answer fell back to id order.
Consequently **every blind figure in this report is stated once**; printing a min/median/max range would print
the same number three times and suggest a measured spread that does not exist. Every count here is permutation
p1, which is sound only because of the above; on a design whose permutations disagreed these counts would have
to be given as a range.

Two caveats attach. "Zero varying metrics" is over the metrics `run.py`'s `headline_metrics()` collects into the
spread block, not literally every number in a score report — though `distinct_answers = 1` does cover the whole
mapped answer, since `canonical_result_hash` hashes each structure as (kind, flops, order, params, outcome
class) plus the sorted groups. And `tools/s3/run.py`'s module docstring records a review of
2026-09-22 finding the recognizer *not* permutation invariant on TEMPO. On the frozen code the TEMPO
record does give one canonical answer across 5 permutations plus the file-order arm, with 0 of 111 metrics
varying — but that is five draws, the argument this paragraph has just refused for the blind set, and the
mechanism that licenses it here does not carry there: every TEMPO arm records `canonical_order`
`tied_cells = 79` and `tied_nets = 79`, against **0 and 0 on all 11 blind/known records**. On TEMPO part of the
answer can still fall back to id order, so the docstring's caution stands for TEMPO and this report does not
call it stale. The docstring is inside the freeze and was not corrected.

**The binding uncertainty is therefore the denominator, not the permutation** (§5.2). With 94 registers a pooled
rate supports at most two significant figures; the third digit in any table above is there to make the
arithmetic checkable, not because it is resolved.

## 15. Limitations

1. **The pre-published out-of-sample estimate is not independent.** The corpus is synthetic and written by this
   project; cohort 2 was added *because* the generalisation regression set showed which classes were missing;
   and **neither** generalisation regression set (`out/s3/review_generalisation`, `…2`) is held out any longer —
   five entries were fitted to five designs of the second one. Its rates come from one seeded evaluation and
   carry no confidence interval. **Two of its own holdout designs were touched.** `RELOAD_DISTINCT = 4` was
   fitted on **`fsm_timer_ar`**, which was in the holdout; `corpus.FITTED_ON` now forces that design into train
   and the holdout rates were re-reported (`changes.jsonl` C41). Four further holdout designs —
   **`tmr_up_autoreload_w16_ar`, `cnt_down_w36_load_ar`, `cnt_case_mod5_ud_ar`, `acc_w8_en_ar`** — are recorded
   as measurements and not fits, but their outcomes were read while the recognizer was being changed, **so the
   holdout is a weaker estimate than an untouched set** (`out/s3/contamination.json` K13).
   **And one holdout design was written from puzzle knowledge.** `contamination.json` K2 records, under its
   `scrambler` family, the design
   **`scr_modes_w8_ar`** — "an 8-bit Fibonacci scrambler with a serial data mode and an 8-step autonomous mode,
   the puzzle scrambler's modes" — with `split: "holdout"` and `on_the_leads_list: false`, and
   `corpus.splits(corpus.designs())` confirms it is one of the 40 (`fsm_timer_ar`, the fitted design above, is
   now `train`). A design shaped by the puzzle therefore sits inside the holdout that every **BELOW** and
   **INDISTINGUISHABLE** verdict in §6 is measured against — most sharply the `lfsr_crc` reference of
   14/14 = 1.000 behind "BELOW (p = 1.2e-06)", and the AMI mean of §12.2. `contamination.json`'s `open` list
   still carries "optionally re-run the corpus evaluation without the K2 families", so this is an acknowledged
   and unclosed path, not a new finding.
   The headline `contamination.json` requires a report to state on this — its top-level
   `puzzle_report_must_state` entry tagged (K13), item 5 of six, not K13's own text — verbatim: "TEMPO's 1.000 is a FITTED
   development figure; the out-of-sample estimate is the
   synthetic corpus holdout, and after this round no uncontaminated generalisation set exists for counter, shift
   or lfsr". It is the best out-of-sample estimate that survives, not an independent one — and it is the
   reference every comparison in §6 is measured against.
2. **The blind set's own denominator is thin and clustered.** 94 registers over 10 designs; only `counter` (59,
   all 10 designs) supports a rate. `lfsr_crc` is 9 registers in one design, `synchronizer` 18 registers that are
   really 4 sites of which 16 are credited through 2 unit matches, `shift_register` 8 registers in 4 designs.
   The intervals and p-values in §6 assume independent registers and are a floor on the uncertainty.
3. **A quarter of the ground truth is not established** (§4): 23 of 94 registers Tier A, 16 of them in one
   design — the same design that supplies every `lfsr_crc` register and 3 of the 8 shift registers. The kind
   that scores 0.000 is measured entirely against labels the labeller's own z3 proof refutes.
4. **The counter certification gap is real and it reproduced.** TEMPO finds 22 of 22 counters and certifies 9;
   the blind set finds 41 of 59 and certifies 23. Every blind found-but-unverified register is the hold
   obligation, and 16 of the 18 are the anti-erasure rule refusing a structure whose hold region only empties
   once an opaque load is conjoined in — correct behaviour, and a ceiling on what this slice can certify.
5. **Certification is not infallible** (§12.1): two certified counter moduli were wrong out of sample. That is
   not a first occurrence — the freeze's own out-of-sample arm has two wrong certified parameters of its own,
   which is what 178/180 means — and it is not a rate either. It is a statement about *naming* two failures: the
   58/60 = 0.967 they make is not separable from the corpus holdout's own certified 178/180 = 0.9889
   (`honesty_table.json`, Fisher two-sided p = 0.26), so the freeze's out-of-sample arm does not support a claim
   that certification degrades on unseen designs.
6. **The blind set is not representative of much.** It reaches 6 of the 14 populated shuttle strata; 9 of 10
   designs are 1×1 tiles and the largest is 385 flops against a candidate pool that runs to 1,656; 9 distinct
   authors, one of whom wrote 2 of the 10 designs supplying 25 of the 94 scored registers including all 9
   `lfsr_crc` and 4 of the 8 shift registers; criterion C5 excludes any design that instantiates a flop or latch
   by hand; all ten designs have 0 latches and no black boxes. The candidate pool's own precedence is
   self-declared: `registered: 2026-09-21` carries day granularity, the file was committed only at the freeze,
   and K7 dates the start of recognizer development to 23:45Z the same day (§3).
7. **Mechanism attribution for the shift_register misses is by shape, not by flop id** (§8.2), and the
   counter-candidate rejection histograms quoted in §8.1 are design-level counts, not per-register
   attributions.
8. **This report explains outcomes; it does not re-run the recognizer.** `tools/s3/score.py` was re-run as an
   imported library over each record's permutation p1 and reproduced the frozen per-kind register counts exactly
   on all 11 records, so the reconstruction is checked. No counterfactual was executed, except where arithmetic
   settles it (§8.3).
9. **One published artifact disagrees with the record it summarises.** `out/s3/honesty/summary.json`'s headline
   sentence says TEMPO scored "macro and micro F1 1.000 on registers (found and exact)" and "106 of 147 exact
   words". The frozen TEMPO record — the artifact the freeze commit force-added — gives found macro/micro F1
   1.000 but **exact** macro 0.9773 / micro 0.9384, and **103** of 147 exact words. This document quotes the
   record. The summary sentence is inside the freeze and was not corrected.
10. **Almost none of the method is new, and the freeze says so.** `out/s3/prior_art.md` (written 2026-09-21,
   force-added in the freeze commit) carries its own "Do not claim as new" list: word grouping by shared control
   signals and by shared predecessor/successor registers (Chisholm '99, Tashjian '15, DANA TCHES '20);
   topology-plus-SAT counter and shift-register detection with aggregation by identical set/reset/enable
   (Subramanyan DATE '13); formal matching that also synthesises the I/O permutation and control conditions
   (Gascón FMCAD '14, WordRev HOST '13); SMT-verified arithmetic with bit order, and bit-order propagation from
   anchors (Klix CCS '24 / HAL); structural LFSR detection with polynomial read-out (Wallat IVSW '17); NMI and
   purity as partition metrics (Meade '18, DANA); placement proximity as a grouping cue (VLSID '23); and
   GDS-to-netlist extraction (ReGDS '20). What this slice offers is the evaluation, not the recognisers: a
   harness that builds each kind's defining template itself and proves it per structure — except for
   `lfsr_crc`, where the recognizer supplies each flop's next-state XOR expression in `proof.claims` and the
   harness checks that expression against D and against its own structural constraints (§1), and where both
   blind polynomial certifications sit — scored on third-party
   sign-off layouts with found and verified reported apart — which the survey lists as a gap "in what I found",
   adding that absence from a search is not proof of absence. The survey also records that it found no paper on
   recognising CRC units in netlists, and calls that a negative search result rather than proof that none
   exists.
